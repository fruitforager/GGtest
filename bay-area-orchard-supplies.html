Pruners, Grafting Knives, Loppers & Sharpeners

The tools Bay Area growers actually reach for — the ones that hold an edge, feel right in your hand, and outlast everything else in the shed.

Precision Tool Specialist Berkeley
Hida Tool & Hardware
📍 1333 San Pablo Ave, Berkeley · (510) 524-3700 · Mon–Sat 10–5

Founded 1982. The Bay Area's destination for authentic Japanese hand tools — forged carbon steel pruners, grafting knives, folding saws, and bonsai tools made using techniques descended from samurai sword-smithing. Edge retention on their carbon steel has no American retail equivalent. Professional sharpening offered (drop Mon, pick up Wed). Customers include Merritt College's horticulture program and serious orchardists, arborists, and backyard fruit growers across Northern California.

Visit site
Professional Standard Global Standard
Felco Pruners
📍 Swiss-made Precision

The benchmark for professional bypass pruners since 1948. Hardened steel blades, every component individually replaceable — a well-maintained Felco lasts decades. The Felco 2 is the classic; the 7 and 8 rotate to reduce wrist strain. Buy once.

Ships Nationwide
A.M. Leonard
📍 Piqua, OH Wholesale Hub

Professional orchard-grade supplier. Full Felco line including replacement parts, plus grafting knives, budding strips, grafting tape, plant labels, and orchard ladders. The go-to for professional supplies unavailable locally.

Budget-Friendly
Zenport Industries
📍 Multi-Tool Manufacturing

Value-priced grafting knives, budding blades, grafting tape, and pruners. A sensible starting point for growers new to grafting before committing to premium Japanese tools.

Compost, Soil Mixes & Amendments

Good fruit starts underground. Find the bulk compost, living amendments, and soil blends that serious local growers haul home by the yard.

Organic Bulk Supplier San Carlos
Lyngso Garden Materials
📍 345 Shoreway Rd, San Carlos · Mon–Sat 7–4

Family-run, employee-owned since 1953. Renowned for their organic soil line: actively aerated compost tea, specialty composts, humus blends, and mulches. Staff trained in soil biology. Bulk pickup or delivery. Runs on 100% renewable energy. The Peninsula grower's first call for serious soil work.

Visit site
North Bay Hub Organic Since 1980
Harmony Farm Supply & Nursery
📍 Sebastopol & Petaluma Yards

Their specialized amendment catalog covers high-quality organic fertilizers, native cover crop seed arrays, beneficial insect releases, and ecological pest management systems. Irrigation blueprinting services on site. One of the most comprehensive organic orchard networks in Northern California.

Visit site
Multiple Locations
American Soil & Stone
📍 Richmond & San Rafael Yards

Bulk landscape and soil building materials across multiple regional yards. The perfect choice for scaling up into serious yardage of compost, clean topsoil blends, and aggregate amendments when single-bag retail options stop making financial sense.

Drip Systems & Water Hardware

Set it up right and your trees water themselves all summer. These are the drip suppliers Bay Area growers trust to keep roots deep and water bills sane.

Irrigation Specialist Ships Nationwide
DripWorks
📍 Willits, CA · (800) 522-3747

California-based drip irrigation specialist since 1991, featuring a massive physical facility and demonstration layouts in Willits. Orchard-specific kits sized cleanly for 20- or 40-tree operations, inclusive of pro-grade regulators, automation timers, and micro-filters. Highly technical staff capable of walking through complex custom properties.

Regional Supplier 7 Bay Area Hubs

📍 San Rafael, Petaluma, Concord, Dublin, San Jose

The only wholesale irrigation supplier owned and operated exclusively within the SF Bay Area — seven supply yards spanning Marin, Sonoma, Contra Costa, Alameda, and Santa Clara. Commercial-grade pressure lines and smart irrigation components at wholesale pricing tiers, fully accessible to home gardeners. Free local delivery with no baseline order minimum.

The Best Local Nurseries to Walk Into

Show up, look around, talk to someone who's actually grown the tree you're eyeing. These are the Bay Area's best independent nurseries — the ones worth making a trip for.

Organic Variety Collection East Oakland Certified Organic
Planting Justice Nursery
📍 319 105th Ave, Oakland · Weekdays 8:30–4, Sat 10–4

Nonprofit nursery based in East Oakland carrying an incredible catalog of 1,100+ certified organic fruit tree varieties — making it one of the largest physical public collections in North America. High-value heritage apples, Asian pears, custom figs, jujubes, cold-hardy loquats, unique pomegranates, and specialized coastal subtropicals. Direct legacy custodians of the famed Rolling River Nursery mother stock assets. Nationwide shipping also active.

Visit site
Established Nursery Berkeley
Berkeley Horticultural Nursery
📍 1310 McGee Ave, Berkeley · Since 1922

Established 1922. The East Bay's landmark independent nursery center, retaining an expert Master Fruit Taster on staff and leveraging a 35+ year custom pipeline for bare-root choices from Dave Wilson. Their highly anticipated mid-winter bare-root releases and annual January pruning workshops are staple events for the local gardening community.

Visit site
Berkeley Legacy Yard
East Bay Nursery
📍 2332 San Pablo Ave, Berkeley · Since 1926

Third-generation family-managed horticultural business serving the region since 1926. Solid structural inventory of foundational fruit trees, micro-climate citrus choices, and annual edibles backed by highly experienced staff and rich premium organic soil builders. Open Tue–Sat. A highly reliable, slightly quieter destination option just a few blocks away.

Visit site
Regional Garden Center Cupertino
Yamagami's Garden Center
📍 1361 S De Anza Blvd, Cupertino · Since 1948

Originally established as a local agricultural fruit stand in 1948. The South Bay's premier garden destination — carrying an extensive Dave Wilson deciduous inventory curated monthly, alongside healthy figs, structural guavas, jujubes, and rare fruiting shrubs. Retains full-time fruit specialists on site. Essential for Peninsula and South Bay fruit growers.

Visit site
Worth the Drive Richmond
Golden Gate Palms & Exotics
📍 425 Cutting Blvd, Richmond

Northern California's largest specialized subtropical botanical plant center. Spans 100+ palm variations, unique cold-tolerant avocado strains, specialized citrus, and select mango specimens pre-climated to stand up to cool maritime microclimates. The primary destination for expanding into multi-zone tropical cultivation. Walk-in retail operations only.

Great Trees Shipped to Your Door

Can't find it locally? These shippers carry the cultivars worth waiting for — heritage varieties, clean genetics, and rootstocks you won't see at any retail nursery. Most ship bare-root January through late March.

Dwarf Citrus Pioneer Ships Nationwide
Four Winds Growers
📍 Watsonville Propagation Grounds · Since 1948

Fourth-generation family dwarf citrus propagation pioneers working since 1948. The state's premier supplier for semi-dwarf and container-ready citrus stock — over 60 varieties coupled with select avocados, high-fruiting blueberries, and olives. Local pickup available by arrangement in Fremont and Watsonville. Premier boutique retail spaces throughout the Bay carry their signature stock lines.

Visit site
Heirloom Specialist Certified Organic
Trees of Antiquity
📍 Paso Robles, CA · Ships Jan–Apr

Formerly operating out of Sonoma County as the Antique Apple Nursery with over 40 years of certified organic heirloom tree experience. Features an extensive collection of 400+ varieties including an unmatched historical heirloom apple listing, stone fruit selections, clean custom pluots, and heavy-bearing mulberries. The primary destination for genetic varieties long gone from standard retail nursery pipelines.

Visit site
Ships Jan–Mar
Bay Laurel Nursery
📍 Atascadero, CA Catalog

An expansive catalog covering 375+ specific cultivars of bare-root fruit trees, fruiting vines, and berry bushes. Renowned for providing some of the clearest chill-hour requirements and specific rootstock identity data anywhere online. Ordering queues open annually in early September, with shipments arriving cleanly January through March. Exceptionally strong for custom stone fruits, Asian pears, and fruiting quinces.

Visit site
Propagation Repository USDA Organic
Fruitwood Nursery
📍 Orleans, CA Genetic Repository

The former Rolling River Nursery management group, now focusing exclusively on foundational propagation wood and structural tissue assets. Lists an impressive 251 classic apple cultivars, 63 unique figs, 69 distinct persimmons, and 59 pomegranate choices — delivered as scionwood pieces, cuttings, clean rootstocks, and small liners. Consistently recommended by regional horticulturalists for growers who are serious about custom propagation and grafting.

Visit site
Ships Nationwide
One Green World
📍 Molalla, OR Import Specialties

Incredible selection depth focusing heavily on unusual fruiting plants — rare fruiting berries, cold-hardy citrus alternatives, obscure stone fruits, and specialty heavy mulberries that are tough to source locally. Packed with reliable, actionable growth profiles and highly practical choices for Northern California coastal climates.

Seeds & Companion Plants

Round out your orchard with the plants that make everything else grow better — ground covers, nitrogen fixers, and companion flowers that pull their weight all season long.

Territorial Seed Company
📍 Cottage Grove, OR Hub

Pacific Coast regional seed house highlighting strong open-pollinated lines and heirloom strains highly acclimated to cool, fog-influenced summer timelines. A beautiful geographic fit for coastal fruit growers looking for beneficial orchard ground covers, soil builders, understory perennial berries, and dynamic companion flowers.

An outstanding index of rare edible herbs, dynamic companion insectaries, and rare perennial support lines provided as pure seed stock or early vegetative plugs.
